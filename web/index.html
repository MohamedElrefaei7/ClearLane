What this map shows

  • Each hexagon (~0.1 km²) on the bike network is coloured by how often a blocked bike lane is reported to NYC 311 there at the chosen day and hour, as expected reports per week at that hour.
  • It measures reports, not obstruction. How often people report depends on who rides, who reports and who knows 311 exists, which varies a lot by neighbourhood.
  • Predicted: a gradient-boosted Poisson model of recent history, lanes, Citi Bike activity and land use, recalibrated to the last three months' level.
  • Reporting-adjusted*: predicted divided by how much the area calls 311 about anything else. A rough heuristic for "risk given how much this area reports", not a measurement.
  • Historical: what was actually reported in that hour over the last 12 months, with no model.
  • Grey hexagons have no protected or painted bike lane.
